Refractory Manufacturer & Exporter from India

JK Refractory manufactures and supplies high alumina bricks, refractory castables, mortars and custom shapes for industrial applications. With over 35 years of industry experience, we support overseas trading companies and end users with consistent quality, technical understanding and export-ready supply.

Engineering-Driven Refractory Solutions

Our operations are based in India, with refractory supplies executed across the North, South, East and West zones. This nationwide operational exposure enables us to support export requirements with stable manufacturing, disciplined quality control and secure packing for international shipments.

Manufacturing Capability

Production of high alumina refractory bricks, dense and insulating castables, mortars and grogs. Custom shapes manufactured as per drawings and specifications.

Quality & Consistency

Controlled raw material selection, batch consistency and inspection practices suitable for both domestic and export supply requirements.

Export Support

Experience with export documentation, palletized and container-safe packing, and coordination for FOB, CIF and EXW shipments.
