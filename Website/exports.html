Exports

JK Refractory supplies refractory products for export requirements with a focus on consistent quality, controlled packing and clear documentation. We work with overseas trading companies and industrial end users seeking reliable refractory supply from India.

Export Readiness

  • Valid Import Export Code (IEC)
  • Experience in executing export consignments
  • Grade identification and product traceability
  • Palletized and container-safe packing
  • Coordination for FOB, CIF and EXW shipments
  • Support for standard export documentation

Product Identification & Traceability

Refractory bricks and shapes can be supplied with clear grade identification and markings to support traceability, quality control and ease of handling at destination. Product marking practices help ensure clarity during inspection, storage and installation.

Export Packing & Handling

Products are packed using palletized and secure methods suitable for domestic and international transportation. Packing practices are designed to minimize breakage and facilitate safe container loading during export shipments.

Documentation Support

JK Refractory supports standard export documentation including commercial invoice, packing list and coordination for certificate of origin as required. Documentation support is provided to ensure smooth customs clearance and shipment processing.

Who We Work With

  • Overseas trading companies sourcing refractories from India
  • Industrial end users requiring consistent refractory materials
  • EPC contractors and project-based suppliers
